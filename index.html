Are Xadboom pieces truly limited in production? −
Yes. Every piece is fabricated in strictly limited, numbered series (typically 6 to 20 units worldwide). Once an edition closes, it is never reissued in the same specification.
Can architects and interior designers download 3D models and tear sheets? +
Yes. Verified design professionals can download high-resolution 3D models (.OBJ, .FBX, .3DM, .IFC) and project-tailored tear sheet documentation directly from asset pages.
How does the checkout reservation lock work? +
To protect low-volume runs during high-demand releases, adding an asset to cart holds that specific serial number for 15 minutes while you finalize your order.
Can we request custom finishes or bespoke dimensions? +
Yes. Select pieces can be commissioned with customized stone, wood, or textile selections through direct coordination with our curatorial team.
Where are items shipped and what are the lead times? +
We provide insured white-glove delivery across Iran, with international freight available upon request. Limited in-stock pieces ship in 5–7 days; made-to-order editions follow verified lead times.
