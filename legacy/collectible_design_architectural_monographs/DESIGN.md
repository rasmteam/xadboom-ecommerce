## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the material sensibility of high-end interior architecture and limited-edition collectible design objects. The identity speaks to collectors, interior architects, and design connoisseurs who value stillness, material authenticity, and tectonic precision. 

The aesthetic is grounded in warm architectural minimalism. Rather than using stark clinical digital surfaces, the UI acts as an uninterrupted wall of lime-wash plaster. Visual hierarchy is established not through layered card stacks or noisy drop shadows, but through structural typographic proportion, deliberate negative space, razor-sharp hairline framing, and intense contrast between mineral alabaster and deep obsidian stone.

## Colors

The core canvas utilizes an unbroken warm plaster neutral (`#ECDAD4`), which remains continuous across all viewports from the highest global navigation bar down through the master footer. Under no circumstances should horizontal bands of cold `#FFFFFF` or synthetic gray disrupt this plane.

- **Primary (`#0A0A0A` / Obsidian)**: Serves as the primary focal point, reserved for primary CTA surfaces, active selection tokens, solid iconography, and authoritative titles.
- **Secondary (`#292524` / Basalt)**: Secondary body copy, subtle interactive hovers, and technical meta text.
- **Tertiary (`#8C7A74` / Terracotta Earth)**: Curatorial notations, catalog sequence tags, dimension measurements, and provenance dates.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#ECDAD4` / Lime Plaster)**: The foundational substrate. Elevated card surfaces do not turn white; they utilize micro-tinted alabaster variants (`#FFF1EC`, `#FAECE7`, or `#FFF8F6`) with low opacity to preserve tactile continuity.
- **Architectural Dividers (`#D8C2BA` / `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.08)`)**: Hairline-weighted borders that frame objects like architectural elevation schematics.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs two distinct traditions: modern tectonic geometry and archival editorial literature.

- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Clean, low-contrast, architectural proportions. Used for major collection titling, scale indicators, and numerical indexing.
- **Monograph Narratives & Curatorial Quotes (`Newsreader`)**: Expressive, classical editorial serif with subtle literary calligraphic cuts. Reserved exclusively for essays, artist signatures, material statements, and object provenance.
- **Body & Specifications (`DM Sans`)**: Utilitarian, highly legible modern sans for structural dimension tables, descriptions, and operational text.
- **Labels & Metas (`Space Grotesk Uppercase`)**: Tracked out aggressively (`+0.12em`) to echo industrial blueprint documentation and gallery catalog entries.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a 12-column architectural grid with generous structural gutters. Layout elements align strictly to structural construction lines rather than floating arbitrarily.

- **Grid Architecture**: 12 columns on desktop with `2.5rem` gutters and minimum `4rem` outer canvas padding; 6 columns on tablet with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` margin; 4 columns on mobile with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margin.
- **Continuous Ground Plane**: Sections do not terminate with background color cuts. Spatial grouping is communicated through vertical negative space (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) and crisp single-pixel hairline grid dividers (`#D8C2BA`).
- **Asymmetric Tension**: Imagery of collectible objects should alternate between structural full-column spans (e.g. 7 columns wide) juxtaposed against generous empty columns (e.g. 5 columns of pure `#ECDAD4` negative space containing right-aligned monograph text).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects simulated atmospheric drop shadows and blur-heavy artificial lighting. In architectural monographs, depth is physical, tactile, and planar.

- **Planar Tiering**: Depth is achieved strictly through tonal warmth. The primary plane is `#ECDAD4`. A resting secondary module sits upon `#FAECE7` or `#FFF1EC`. An activated or focused element sits upon `#FFF8F6`.
- **Architectural Hairlines**: All elevation borders use a `1px` crisp rule in `#D8C2BA` or `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.08)`. Shadows are replaced entirely with these architectural joints.
- **Overlapping Relief**: When objects overlap in catalog lookbooks, depth is established via high-resolution transparent PNG cutouts casting their own organic studio shadow directly onto the plaster background, never via CSS box-shadows.
- **Focus States**: High-contrast, sharp obsidian stroke rings (`#0A0A0A` at `1.5px`) offset by `2px` of pure canvas bleed.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary is uncompromisingly crisp and tectonic (`roundedness: 0`). Elements favor precise 90-degree corners reminiscent of chiseled stone, milled aluminum, and architectural blueprints.

- **Containers, Framing, and Inputs**: Strict `0px` radius. Sharp edges highlight the architectural discipline and material seriousness of the collectible pieces.
- **Pill Exceptions**: Small taxonomic interactive chips and active curatorial tag filters may optionally employ full circular capsule caps (`rounded-full`) to contrast sharply against strictly rectilinear structural framing modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid obsidian background (`#0A0A0A`), crisp text (`#ECDAD4`), `0px` border radius, padding `1rem 2rem`. Text set in Space Grotesk `label-caps`. Hover transitions smoothly to basalt (`#292524`).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent background over `#ECDAD4`, framed with a `1px` border of `#0A0A0A`. Hover inverts to solid `#0A0A0A` with `#ECDAD4` text.
- **Text / Inline Action**: Bare obsidian typography accompanied by a hairline right arrow glyph (`→`), underlined by a hairline rule spaced `4px` beneath the baseline.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Rectilinear or pill-shaped filters. Inactive state: `#ECDAD4` background with `1px` hairline `#D8C2BA` border and `#292524` text. Active state: Solid obsidian (`#0A0A0A`) with alabaster (`#FFF8F6`) text.

### Cards & Object Display Cells
- Cards do not use white backgrounds. They utilize `#FFF1EC` or border-only containment over `#ECDAD4`.
- Imagery is treated as an archival plate: raw, unclipped, surrounded by generous inner breathing room (`space-lg`), anchored by an uppercase catalog index (e.g., `OBJ-048 / BRONZE`) in `label-caps`.

### Lists & Tables
- Border-separated using `1px` horizontal hairlines (`#D8C2BA`). No vertical table borders.
- Monospaced or tabular numbers in Space Grotesk for dimensions, edition numbers, and prices. Left column curatorial details set in Newsreader italics.

### Input Fields
- Flat translucent plaster wells (`#FAECE7`) with a single bottom hairline rule (`1px` `#0A0A0A`) or full razor-bordered frame (`1px` `#D8C2BA`). 
- Focus transforms the border to an intense obsidian `#0A0A0A`. Labels float above in Space Grotesk `label-caps`.

### Curatorial Monograph Callouts
- Wide-column editorial blocks rendered in Newsreader (`curatorial-quote`), flanked on the left edge by a `1px` `#8C7A74` vertical rule, attributing the craftsperson or collector with zero box containment.
