03 / Collaborating Monograph & Biography/Architectural Retrospective

Farshid Moussavi / FMA

Practice: London · Tehran · Harvard GSD
In-Situ Architectural Retrospective · Gallery Villa Lavasan
Curatorial Commission 2026

Architectural Envelope Geometries Translated to Monolithic Collectible Scale

Farshid Moussavi OBE RA is an internationally acclaimed architect, educator, and author. Founder of Farshid Moussavi Architecture (FMA) and Professor in Practice of Architecture at Harvard University Graduate School of Design, her seminal trajectory spans pioneering masterworks including the Yokohama International Port Terminal and the Museum of Contemporary Art Cleveland.

In her ongoing collaboration with Xadboom Atelier, Moussavi translates the rigorous micro-tectonics of tessellated ceramic facade systems into heavy monolithic furniture objects. Each limited piece embodies spatial mutability, balancing geometric exactitude with hand-cast bronze and tactile subterranean travertine.
