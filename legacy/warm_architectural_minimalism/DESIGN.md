## Brand & Style
The Tectonic Atelier design system embodies architectural discipline, collectible industrial design, and gallery curation. It serves architects, spatial designers, and discerning design patrons seeking limited-run physical assets and precise architectural specifications.

The aesthetic fuses **Architectural Minimalism** with **Editorial Brutalism**:
- Warm, tactile, stone-derived surface palettes replace sterile stark whites.
- Micro-hairline structural framing and strict grid alignment reflect physical blueprints and spatial joinery.
- High-contrast, austere dark typography paired with technical monospace labels delivers authoritative clarity without superfluous decoration.

## Colors
The color palette derives from raw architectural materials: porous travertine, untreated ash, terracotta plaster, and oxidized cast bronze.

- **Canvas & Backdrops:** The foundational canvas is `#ECDAD4`, providing a warm, non-glare architectural backdrop across whole-page compositions.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** Elevated interactive areas, cards, and input fields use `#FFF1EC` and `#FFF8F6`, subtly lifting assets against the canvas.
- **Structural Dividing Lines:** Precise hairlines rendered in `#D8C3BC` and `#E2CECB` create planar structure instead of drop shadows.
- **Typographic Ink:** Primary text is anchor-dark `#1C1917` down to `#0A0A0A`, with secondary metadata and technical indicators dialed to `#7A6E69`.
- **Editorial Accents:** Muted terracotta `#844C38` and sculpted clay rose `#C99187` highlight special editions and archival hero installations.

## Typography
Typography is divided between balanced modernist geometric sans and technical architectural notation:

- **DM Sans:** Used for all titles, narrative editorial blocks, and button labels. Display titles employ tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.035em` to `-0.06em`) to echo the disciplined composition of architectural title blocks.
- **Space Grotesk:** Reserved exclusively for indices, technical specs (BIM formats, dimensions, batch availability), step counters, and system labels. All monospace labels are rendered uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.1em`).
- **Arrows and Indices:** Direct directional indicators (`↗`, `←`, `→`) are integrated inline to denote architectural drawing calls and specimen expansion.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to strict architectural planes framed by maximum horizontal constraints:

- **Max Width:** Content is capped at `1360px`, centered within responsive margins (`1.5rem` on mobile scaling to `3rem` on desktop).
- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column architectural grid divides content for large viewports (e.g., a 4-column descriptor paired with an 8-column proposition), collapsing gracefully to 1 or 2 columns on mobile devices.
- **Section Rhythm:** Major sections use generous vertical breathing room (`5rem` / `space-2xl`) defined by continuous horizontal hairline borders (`border-t`, `border-b`) rather than heavy background switches.
- **Internal Micro-Spacing:** Compact item gaps (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) keep labels and spec tokens grouped tightly with their respective fields.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in the Tectonic Atelier system avoids skeuomorphic drops and heavy ambient blurs. Elevation is conveyed strictly through planar layering:

- **Planar Hairline Division:** Structural boundaries are established with `1px` hairlines (`#D8C3BC`). Cards and list items rely on single-pixel division rather than drop shadows.
- **Subtle Surface Tinting:** Cards and modules float by shifting tone from the foundation canvas (`#ECDAD4`) to delicate tinted surfaces (`#FFF1EC` at 80% opacity).
- **Glassmorphic Floating Overlays:** Image tags, badge overlays, and the sticky site header employ semi-transparent dark or light tints (`bg-black/40` or `bg-brand-canvas/90`) paired with `backdrop-blur-md`.
- **Shadow Elimination:** Standard boxes use `shadow-none`. Floating primary CTA buttons and featured asset cards carry at most a feather-weight `shadow-sm` (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`).

## Shapes
Geometry is disciplined and architectural, favoring soft structural corners balanced against pure pill buttons:

- **Cards & Image Containers:** Soft corner radius of `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) and `0.75rem` (`rounded-xl` for major banners) softens high-contrast industrial photography without feeling bubbly.
- **Buttons & Search Inputs:** Complete pill geometry (`rounded-full`) creates tactile contrast with the rectilinear card grid.
- **Tags & Floating Badges:** Micro-radii of `0.25rem` (`rounded`) ensure badges resemble stamped physical brass plates or specimen archive tape.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Full rounded (`rounded-full`), solid `#0A0A0A` background with crisp white typography, tracked uppercase `0.08em`. Hover states gently shift to `#262626`.
- **Secondary Ghost / Link:** Underlined `#1C1917` text or inline monospace link ending with arrow glyph (`↗`), operating with `0.75` opacity hover transitions.
- **Icon / Arrow Steppers:** `32px` circular buttons with hairline borders (`#E2CECB`) or filled solid black, used for gallery carousels and detail toggles.

### Cards & Asset Tiles
- **Aspect Ratios:** Strict geometric framing using `aspect-[3/4]`, `aspect-[4/3]`, and `aspect-[21/9]` for wide hero displays.
- **Badges:** Absolute positioned top/bottom badges with dark backdrop blurs (`rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.8)` with `backdrop-blur-sm`), displaying edition numbering (e.g., `Ed. 03 / 12`).
- **Interactive State:** Hover initiates an image scale transform (`scale-105` over `700ms`) with smooth transition curves.

### Specification Rows & Accordions
- **List Rows:** Divided by `1px solid #D8C3BC`. When hovered, the spec arrow (`↗`) translates `3px` northeast while maintaining steady baseline alignment.
- **Accordions / FAQs:** Minimalist `<details>` summaries stripping default disclosure markers. Rotates a delicate glyph (`+` to `−` or `45deg`) on state change.

### Inputs & Formulary
- **Newsletter / Inquiry Fields:** Full-bleed pill inputs (`rounded-full`) backed by `#FFF1EC`, bordered by hairline `#E2CECB`. Active focus rings are discarded in favor of crisp `#1C1917` border strokes. Monospace placeholder styling.

### Progress & Status Trackers
- **Batch / Process Trackers:** Micro-thin horizontal tracks (`4px` height) with `#E5D2CB` troughs and dark ink fill bars indicating edition progress (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%).
