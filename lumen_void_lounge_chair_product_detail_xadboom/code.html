SECTION 01 / CURATORIAL ESSAY

The Tectonic Concept: Dynamic Cantilever

“The void beneath the seat is not empty space; it is structural tension held in delicate equilibrium by hand-carved Iranian walnut.”

Alireza Taghaboni • NextOffice Studio, Tehran

Commissioned by Xadboom for the 2024 Architectural Editions cycle, the Lumen Void Lounge Chair interrogates the boundary between building-scale tectonics and body-scale ergonomics. Taghaboni draws directly from the kinetic principles pioneered in his celebrated Sharifi-ha House, translating spatial reconfigurability into a static object that feels poised for motion.

The rear stiles curve downward through an aggressive rake, sweeping into a ground-tethered wishbone cantilever. Instead of four standard upright legs, the chair redistributes occupant mass through double-lap tenons concealed within the sculpted walnut armatures. The resulting posture suspends the sitter 415 mm above the floor plane with zero visual clutter beneath the main perimeter.
